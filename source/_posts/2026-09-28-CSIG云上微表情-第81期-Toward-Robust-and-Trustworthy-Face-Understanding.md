微表情是一种短暂的、微弱的、无意识的面部微表情，持续时间往往在0.5s内，能够揭示人类试图隐藏的真实情绪。微表情识别的研究旨在让机器有足够的智能，能够从人脸视频序列中识别人类的真实情绪。然而由于微表情持续时间短、面部肌肉运动强度低，对其进行准确的表征与识别是一项极具挑战性的任务。为了促进心理学领域和计算机视觉领域针对微表情的进一步研究，由中国图象图形学学会（CSIG）和中国科学院心理研究所举办、CSIG机器视觉专业委员会、CSIG情感计算与理解专业委员会、CSIG生物特征识别专委会和中科院青促会心理所小组联合承办，中国科学院心理研究所的王甦菁博士和李婧婷博士组织一系列云上微表情的学术活动。
 
第八十一期云上微表情于2026年09月28日晚7点进行，由中国科学院心理研究所王甦菁老师团队的李婧婷博士主持。此次讲座由台湾清华大学助理教授罗婈博士带来主题为“Toward Robust and Trustworthy Face Understanding”的学术报告，欢迎大家关注！

个人简介：
罗婈博士，现为台湾清华大学资讯工程学系助理教授，于台湾阳明交通大学取得电子工程学士及博士学位，并曾赴美国加州大学默塞德分校（UC Merced）与日本东京大学进行访问研究。研究领域主要涵盖情感计算、计算机视觉、生成式人工智能及可信视觉内容生成，研究成果发表于 CVPR、ICCV、AAAI、ACM MM、IEEE TMM 及 IEEE TAFFC 等国际会议与期刊，并曾获 IEEE ICME 2021 最佳论文奖。

报告摘要： Human faces contain some of the most important visual signals for understanding people, conveying subtle information about their affect and behavior. At the same time, the rapid development of generative AI is fundamentally changing how facial content can be created and perceived. Together, these developments raise a broader question: how can we reliably understand facial evidence when it may be subtle, incomplete, or even synthetically manipulated?
This talk presents our recent efforts toward robust and trustworthy face understanding from two complementary perspectives. First, we study micro-expression recognition in real-world occlusion scenarios, where subtle expression cues can be easily obscured. We introduce an approach that explicitly identifies informative micro-expression regions, improving robustness to occlusions while enabling targeted data augmentation without relying on unconstrained synthetic generation. We then turn to DeepFake detection in the era of generative AI, asking whether models can not only distinguish real from fake, but also correctly perceive manipulation evidence and provide explanations grounded in observable artifacts. Together, we aim to explore how facial AI can move toward a more reliable understanding of both authentic human signals and synthetic facial content.
